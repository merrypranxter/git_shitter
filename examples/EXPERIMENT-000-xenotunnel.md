# EXAMPLE EXPERIMENT 000 — THE XENOTUNNEL HAS NO INSIDE

**Status:** Hand-authored specimen showing the crossbreed quality bar. NOT an automated harvest, and NOT a claim that the Klein fluid repository already implements non-orientable topology.

**PARENTS:** [slime_molds](https://github.com/merrypranxter/slime_molds) × [klein-fluid-sim](https://github.com/merrypranxter/klein-fluid-sim) × [LV426](https://github.com/merrypranxter/LV426)

**SOURCE DNA:**

- `slime_molds`: Documented plasmodial growth, branching visual taxonomy, cellular swarms and lifecycle transformations.
- `klein-fluid-sim`: Documented only as a fluid-dynamics playground. A Klein-bottle-like non-orientable surface is a proposed artistic mathematical lens, **not a verified software capability**.
- `LV426`: A source library about the Alien franchise; supplies a biomechanical alien-environment aesthetic. This prompt's living tunnel behavior is original creative extrapolation.

**FUSION LOGIC:** Imagine a slime-mold transport network colonizing a topological surface with no consistent inside/outside designation, then reinterpret the network as the circulation of an alien tunnel system. Arteries that leave one apparent chamber re-enter an inverted neighboring chamber. The structure's holes, membranes and thick paths must communicate this inversion through graphic connections, not 3D perspective. The mismatch between local biological growth and global topological orientation becomes the design engine.

## READY-TO-USE DINGBAT IMAGE PROMPT

Create a sheet of 9 **completely independent** black mathematical-organic dingbat ornaments that resemble living xenotunnels whose interior and exterior cannot agree on which side is which. Each ornament grows from one of three shape grammars: thick slime-mold transport veins that reconnect after an impossible orientation reversal; bulbous hollow organisms with a fat black outer loop and multiple broad white chambers arranged on mutually contradictory sides of the loop; or compact alien corridor structures where three branches bend toward a central passage only to re-emerge as four outward-facing tubes. Use walled channels, oversized organic valves, abrupt geometric inversions, large white tunnels and occasional blunt mechanical teeth; make every outer silhouette unique. Allow the sense of a Klein-bottle-like passage to be an optical metaphor only. This is experimental invented topology, not a mathematical diagram. Every design must remain bold and legible when reduced to a keyboard glyph.

Place all 9 designs on one perfectly pure white (#FFFFFF) canvas, each at roughly similar scale and separated by generous white gaps so they can be individually traced in Illustrator. Artwork entirely solid pure black (#000000), large solid masses, crisp closed contours and clean unfilled white voids. Strictly flat 2D. No gradients, grays, soft edges, shadow, lighting, texture, halftones, thin lines, figures, people, landscapes, lettering, equations, labels, visible grids, boxes, borders or touching symbols. Aesthetic: deranged mathematical parasites meet 1970s psychedelic vinyl stickers, with technically motivated impossible connectivity.

**WHY IT'S WEIRD:** Ordinary networks solve paths in their environment. These networks appear to be constructing an environment that makes the distinction between a path and its enclosing wall ambiguous.
