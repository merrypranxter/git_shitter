# foil_sticker_aesthetics — REPO SEED
Status: SPECIFICATION ONLY; style and optical-finish vocabulary, not a real commercial sticker printing proof.

## The Goldmine
Y2K die-cut sticker graphics, simulated diffraction foil, embossed sparkle borders, transparent jelly highlights, and exaggerated fake holographic laminates. The goal is to derive *reusable finish controls* that could be applied to an SVG/dingbat silhouette or animated text, not just collect adjectives.

## The optical caveat
Real diffraction foil has wavelength- and angle-dependent effects. A flat screen image is a stylized simulation unless it includes a physical view/light model. Separate: (a) graphic rainbow ramp, (b) angle/normal-driven iridescent approximation, (c) fixed procedural sparkle, (d) drop-shadow/emboss illusion. Do not mislabel all gradients as scientifically accurate structural color.

## Surface grammar
- **Carrier**: die-cut silhouette or imported text/SVG mask, with 2–8px apparent edge radius and optional sticker-white keyline.
- **Foil**: sharp ribbons of saturated hue, narrow high-intensity prismatic streaks, spectral grain; angle/phase controls.
- **Embossing**: slight offset bright rim + opposed dark rim, subtle at small scale, no gray muddy fill unless selected.
- **Sparkle**: sparse star glints with deterministic positions and animated specular travel (readable at 64–256 px).
- **Print imperfections**: isolated lamination bubbles, hairline registration shifts, foil dropouts, adhesive curl; optional, not always on.
- **Maximalist kitsch**: combine glitter with chrome and hot neon colors, but maintain clear icon silhouettes.

## Reusable pipeline
1. Accept input alpha mask or SVG from `daddydingy`, `sparklebae`, `guilloche_engine` or a future glyph generator.
2. Compute inner/outer distance masks (SDF where possible).
3. Generate color phase field for foil; optionally derive from 2D normal or height approximation.
4. Apply edge rims and sparse star glints in separate passes.
5. Composite output with transparent background and optional die-cut white border.
6. Export PNG; export GIF/MP4 only when animation runs in existing rendering/export tooling.

## Aesthetic regimes
- `alien_holo_decal`: neon green UFO head with foil crown and oversized keyline.
- `mall_sticker_1998`: jelly highlight + stepped pearlescent bands.
- `micro_chrome`: crisp metallic lettering with minimal edge effects.
- `illegally_shiny`: intentionally exaggerated animated chromatic sweeps and glint explosions (artistic effect, not physical claim).

## Existing repos
Prioritize reusing `structural_color`, `thin_film_iridescence`, `sparkles`, `shiny`, `sparklebae` and `color_cycling` rather than rewriting their effects. This repo should mainly **orchestrate style presets** around shared render primitives. Later use as a modular finish for `sticker_slop` or commercial forks only after separation and testing.

## Tests / win condition
Input mask alpha must survive transparent PNG export; same input+seed+phase reproduces identical output; 64 px icon stays legible; optional animated glints preserve masks between frames. Sample presets have screenshots and parameter manifests.

## Closing directive
Audit existing reflective/glitter code and implement an adapter for one proven finish before adding new shaders. First deliverable: apply 3 clearly different foil/sticker treatments to the *same* SVG dingbat with transparent PNG export, without paid image-generation calls.
