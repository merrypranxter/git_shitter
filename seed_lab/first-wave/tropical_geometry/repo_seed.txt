# tropical_geometry — REPO SEED
Status: SPECIFICATION ONLY.

## The Goldmine
A different geometry of sharp branches, polyhedral ridges and polygonal domains, based on real tropical algebra rather than fake "angular fractal" keywords. Build corner-locus geometry from a max-plus polynomial and turn it into alien maps, sigils and animated contour families.

## Mathematical spine
Use max-plus convention: tropical "addition" a⊕b=max(a,b); tropical "multiplication" a⊗b=a+b. A tropical polynomial in two variables is a piecewise-linear convex function
F(x,y) = max_i (a_i*x + b_i*y + c_i)
for finitely many affine forms. Its tropical curve is the *corner locus* where the maximum is attained by at least two distinct forms. The regions where one term uniquely wins are polygonal/polyhedral cells. Do not confuse a smooth usual polynomial plot with a tropical curve. Coincident terms need deduplication and tie handling.

## Pipeline / implementation
1. Input a finite term array [{ax, by, c}] and a clip rectangle.
2. Evaluate winning term and margin to runner-up on a regular pixel field for preview.
3. For accurate SVG: clip planar cells by half-plane inequalities F_i >= F_j; derive shared cell boundaries as line segments; deduplicate edges.
4. Support the dual subdivision idea as educational metadata where available; not essential for MVP.
5. Animate constant coefficients c_i for genuine combinatorial changes (record critical transitions); optionally modulate a separate art overlay with `moire` or `color_fields`.
6. Export polygonal cells, junction nodes and separate vector path groups.
