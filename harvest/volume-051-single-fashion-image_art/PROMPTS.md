## 01 — Space Age Architectural Shift

**BASIS:** styles/space_age_futurism.md

**IMAGE / IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT:**

A hyper-stylized, full-body shot of a model wearing an architectural, A-line shift dress in stark white PVC, featuring exaggerated geometric paneling and a high, sculpted collar. The dress appears to be constructed from rigid, molded plastic, with seams that create sharp, almost impossible angles. Accessorized with opaque white go-go boots and a matching spherical helmet.
