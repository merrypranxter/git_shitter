## 02 — Datamosh Chroma-Shift Orb

**BASIS:** digital compression artifacts, web recompression damage, datamosh residue, chroma bleed

**IMAGE / IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT:**

An animated GIF featuring a perfectly spherical orb of pure white light. As the GIF loops, the orb visibly 'datamoshes' in place, causing blocks of color to detach and smear outwards in short, sharp bursts. The primary effect is a violent, localized chroma-shift where the white light momentarily fractures into jarring RGB splits, particularly around the edges of the displaced blocks. The animation should feel abrupt and digital, with hard edges to the smearing.

**WHY IT'S WEIRD:** Isolates the datamoshing artifact to a single, pure form, exaggerating the chroma-shift and blocky smear as the core visual event.
