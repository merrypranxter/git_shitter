A looping animated GIF of a distorted CRT screen displaying a vibrant, blooming geometric pattern. The pattern itself is a moiré interference effect generated by overlapping digital grids, causing pulsing concentric rings. **WHY IT'S WEIRD:** This asset imagines the failure signature of a digital display component (transistor) as a blooming optical phenomenon, blending analog burn-in with digital grid interference, all packaged as a retro web artifact.
