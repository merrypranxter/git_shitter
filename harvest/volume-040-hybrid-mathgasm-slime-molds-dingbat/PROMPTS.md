# VOLUME 040 — Mathematical Grammars of Living Connectivity

**MODE:** HYBRID · **FORMAT:** dingbat · **DATE:** 2026-11-10

**PARENTS:** [Mathgasm](https://github.com/merrypranxter/Mathgasm) × [slime_molds](https://github.com/merrypranxter/slime_molds)

**HYBRID THESIS:** This project explores the intersection of abstract mathematical structures and the emergent, adaptive connectivity of biological systems, specifically slime molds, rendered as bold, isolated graphic silhouettes. It translates topological invariants and algorithmic processes into the language of adaptive growth and decay, manifesting as distinct glyphs for digital art applications.

## 01 — Plasmodial Network Contours

**BASIS:** The adaptive branching networks of slime molds, particularly the plasmodial stage, visualized as dynamic, interconnected veins. Mathgasm's noise fields and texture synthesis provide a generative framework for creating complex, non-repeating patterns.

**PARENT CONTRIBUTIONS:**

- **Mathgasm:** Utilizing math texture synthesis to generate complex, non-repeating noise fields that serve as the underlying structure for branching pathways. The prompt compiler can be used to define parameters for 'density,' 'branching factor,' and 'connectivity,' translating abstract math concepts into visual primitives.
- **slime_molds:** Drawing from the morphology of plasmodial slime molds (e.g., *Physarum polycephalum*) for the vein-like, interconnected, and adaptive growth patterns. Texture terms like 'mucus-slick,' 'metallic wet,' and 'vascular gel' inform the visual character of the black forms.

**FUSION LOGIC:** Mathgasm's noise fields and prompt generation capabilities are used to procedurally create the intricate, organic branching patterns that are characteristic of slime mold plasmodia. The 'topology' of these generated patterns is then treated as a core property, dictating the fundamental structure of the glyphs, while the 'visual DNA' from slime molds informs the texture and surface details (e.g., 'mucus-slick,' 'metallic wet').

**IMAGE / IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT:**

CREATE A DADDY DINGY DINGBAT SHEET. The nine glyphs must depict abstract representations of slime mold plasmodial network growth. Some glyphs should show dense, interconnected vein-like structures, others should display more sparse, bifurcating pathways. The overall aesthetic should be intricate black linework on a white background, with generous spacing between each glyph. Ensure at least one glyph suggests a Turing pattern originating from a central point, and another resembles a metallic, iridescent oil-slick on a dark background, translated into pure black and white.

MANDATORY DADDY DINGY RENDER CONTRACT (overrides any conflicting formatting or simplification above): Render ONE 1:1 square image with EXACTLY NINE different standalone dingbats on pure white (#FFFFFF). Every mark must be pure black (#000000); white is negative space, not a second paint color. Arrange the nine in a loose INVISIBLE 3x3. Leave generous, entirely empty white clearance horizontally, vertically AND diagonally between every complete motif, including its outermost dots, drips, stars and flourishes. Each glyph should occupy approximately 65-75% of its imaginary cell in its dominant dimension, not reach the cell edges; no rows, columns or neighbors may visually merge. All nine must have distinct silhouettes/internal organization and recognizable focal structure. Intricate thin linework, stippling, engraving-like texture, black dots, cutouts and elaborate ornaments ARE ALLOWED: composition over simplicity. No gray, gradients, simulated glitter, drop shadows, blur, lighting, 3D, visible cell borders, panels, guides, captions, numbers, lettering or watermarks. Outlines and finishes are applied downstream.

**WHY IT'S WEIRD:** We are using the concept of topological invariants (from Mathgasm's focus on mathematical properties) to define the fundamental, unchanging 'shape' of a slime mold's growth network, even as its surface texture and specific branching change. This treats the network's connectivity as a mathematical object with inherent properties, rather than just a biological process.
