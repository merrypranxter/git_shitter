## 01 — Klein Bottle Xenotunnel Network

**BASIS:** Slime mold plasmodia forming adaptive networks, Klein bottle topology for orientation reversal, and LV426 xenotunnel aesthetics.

**PARENT CONTRIBUTIONS:**

- **slime_molds:** Leverages slime mold plasmodial growth patterns and network-forming behavior as the primary constructive agent. - **klein-fluid-sim:** The speculative aspect of Klein bottle topology is used to enforce a non-orientable spatial relationship between interconnected tunnel segments, implying fluid or matter flows that emerge from the 'inside' to the 'outside' of adjacent structures without seam or break. This is a conceptual overlay on the fluid dynamics playground idea.
- **LV426:** The biomechanical, sinuous, and oppressive aesthetic of LV426 xenotunnels serves as the base architectural template and environmental context. The 'gothic pin' and 'metallic iridescent' color themes are subtly integrated into external shell details.

**FUSION LOGIC:** Slime mold branching logic (slime_molds) provides the underlying growth and network-building mechanism. The Klein bottle concept (klein-fluid-sim, speculative) dictates the spatial relationship and orientation reversal between tunnel segments. The LV426 aesthetic (LV426) dictates the overall form and material appearance of the xenotunnels, creating an environment where the slime mold growth conforms to an alien, topologically complex structure.

**IMAGE / IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT:**

Generate a hyperrealistic macro photograph of a network of interconnected xenotunnels. The tunnels should resemble the biomechanical, sinuous forms found on LV426, but their internal surfaces are rendered as pulsating slime mold plasmodia in vibrant 'canary electric' yellow and 'amber translucent' hues. Crucially, the visual rendering must imply a Klein bottle topology: the inside of one tunnel seamlessly transitions to the outside of another without a distinct boundary, creating a disorienting, self-referential loop. The texture should be wet, glistening, and slightly viscous. Use 'sulfur yellow' and 'ochre gold' for external shell details. Lighting should be harsh and directional, emphasizing the alien, industrial-organic fusion.

**WHY IT'S WEIRD:** This prompt merges the self-organizing growth of slime molds with the speculative non-orientability of a Klein bottle, applied to the hostile, biomechanical architecture of the Alien franchise. The resultant structure is a topologically impossible, living tunnel system.
