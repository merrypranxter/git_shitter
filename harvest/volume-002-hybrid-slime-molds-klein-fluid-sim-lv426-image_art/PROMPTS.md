# VOLUME 002 — Non-Euclidean Slime Molds Building Xenotunnels

**MODE:** HYBRID · **FORMAT:** image_art · **DATE:** 2026-10-09

**PARENTS:** [slime_molds](https://github.com/merrypranxter/slime_molds) × [klein-fluid-sim](https://github.com/merrypranxter/klein-fluid-sim) × [LV426](https://github.com/merrypranxter/LV426)

**HYBRID THESIS:** This project crossbreeds the adaptive pathfinding and cellular branching of slime molds, the fluid dynamics playground of a Klein bottle metaphor, and the alien biome aesthetic of Xenomorph architecture to create living, self-assembling xenotunnels that redefine interior/exterior and spatial orientation.

## 01 — Klein-Bottle Plasmodium Conduit

**BASIS:** This prompt envisions a slime mold plasmodium, inspired by its adaptive pathfinding and branching growth from 'slime_molds', actively constructing Xenomorph-like xenotunnels. The 'klein-fluid-sim' repo provides a conceptual lens: the fluid dynamics playground suggests pathways and growth that appear to reverse interior/exterior orientation. The 'LV426' repo contributes the bio-mechanical, xenotunnel aesthetic.

**PARENT CONTRIBUTIONS:**

- **slime_molds:** Provides the foundational mechanism of plasmodial network growth, cellular branching, and adaptive pathfinding, along with texture terms like 'metallic wet', 'bismuth-crystal', 'vascular gel', and 'edge-glow gel' for visual detail.
- **klein-fluid-sim:** Supplies the conceptual framework of non-orientability and reversed orientation, influencing the spatial topology of the xenotunnel network, interpreting 'fluid dynamics' as the flow of building material and topological transformation.
- **LV426:** Defines the alien biome aesthetic of the xenotunnel structure itself, its bio-mechanical form, ribbed textures, and dark, oppressive atmosphere.

**FUSION LOGIC:** The slime mold's plasmodial network growth, capable of intricate pathfinding (from 'slime_molds'), is guided by the non-orientable loop metaphor from 'klein-fluid-sim'. This means the growing network's internal space can seemingly flip to become external, and vice-versa, creating tunnels with intrinsically reversed inside/outside properties. The LV426 xenotunnel aesthetic dictates the form and texture of these self-assembling structures.

**IMAGE / IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT:**

A macro photograph of a viscous, metallic-sheen slime mold plasmodium, resembling molten bismuth-crystal and gunmetal-dark xenomorph plating, actively extruding and weaving itself into a closed, non-orientable xenotunnel. The tunnel's interior surface, which appears to be the exterior and vice-versa, exhibits fractal branching patterns and is coated in a 'vascular gel' texture. The overall structure glows with a faint 'edge-glow gel' from subsurface bioluminescence. Composition: Extreme close-up, tunnel entrance curves into foreground, slight motion blur on extruding tendrils, subject is the actively forming conduit.

**WHY IT'S WEIRD:** The concept of a living tunnel where the inside is also the outside, driven by a slime mold's growth logic interacting with a speculative Klein bottle fluid dynamic metaphor, creating bio-mechanical architecture. The slime mold's 'adaptive pathfinding' is reinterpreted as the traversal of a non-orientable manifold, blurring dimensional boundaries of the structure.

**PARENT CONTRIBUTIONS:**

- **klein-fluid-sim:** Informs the 'self-inverting' property of the vascular networks, creating a topological puzzle for embryo release and transport.
- **LV426:** Provides the 'facehugger' organism morphology and 'acid blood' composition for the fluid, defining the alien biological components.

**FUSION LOGIC:** Slime mold 'spore ash' and 'velvety spores' textures are re-imagined as the reproductive output, but instead of spores, they are microscopic, dormant 'facehugger' embryos. The 'klein-fluid-sim' metaphor suggests these embryo-carrying vascular networks exhibit self-inverting properties, so traversal through them can lead to an 'inside-out' activation of the embryo. The LV426 xenomorph biology informs the viscous, corrosive fluid carrier and the segmented, gripping morphology of the nascent facehugger.

**IMAGE / IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT:**

A cross-section of a pulsating, dark xenotunnel wall, lined with intricate, 'gothic' vascular structures. **WHY IT'S WEIRD:** The idea that a slime mold's reproductive mechanism (spores) is repurposed as a delivery system for Xenomorph embryos, contained within self-inverting vascular networks. This turns a biological defense into a spatial anomaly.

- **klein-fluid-sim:** Introduces the 'non-orientable' property to the forming tunnels, making their interior/exterior boundaries ambiguous and self-reversing, a metaphor for topological breakdown and reformation.
